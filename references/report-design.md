# Report design

Restrained professional styling: A4 or Letter margins and page breaks; neutral sans-serif body text with a clear heading hierarchy; cover page with product, version, assessment period, legal baseline and confidentiality classification; repeating footer with report title, version, page number and the short disclaimer; a compact results table rather than dashboard graphics; gap callouts emphasising rating, provisions and status without heavy colour; tables that repeat headers across pages; appendices for matrices and evidence.

No CE marking symbol anywhere in the report. No EU, Commission, ENISA, notified-body or consulting-firm logos or wording implying affiliation. The manufacturer's own branding only, if any.

## Cross-format consistency

One frozen Markdown source; reconcile cover metadata, counts, every gap and every appendix across Markdown, DOCX and PDF; inspect page breaks, repeated table headers, footers and page numbers; verify long identifiers and URLs do not clip; record output hashes in the rendering manifest.

## Disclaimer treatment

Full disclaimer on the cover or immediately after it in normal body text or a boxed notice, never small print; short forms in the executive summary and conclusion; footer line substantially: `Independent CRA Readiness Assessment | Not a Conformity Assessment | Not Legal Advice`.
