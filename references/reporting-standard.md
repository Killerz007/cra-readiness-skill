# Reporting standard

The formal report must read as a mature assurance deliverable that a product owner can hand to engineering, management, counsel, a notified body or a market surveillance authority, while never implying that it is a conformity assessment, certification or legal opinion.

## Style

Factual, concise, evidence-led; criteria, procedure, observation, risk and recommendation kept distinct; no marketing language; no "fully compliant"; no invented management responses; tables for matrices and registers, prose for analysis; consistent IDs; explicit limitations; the mandatory disclaimers.

## Executive summary

Purpose and scope; product, version, commit and environment; legal baseline with source hashes and the date-sensitive status checks; scope decision and role; classification and available conformity routes; procedures performed; provision results by status; gaps by rating; key themes; readiness conclusion; material limitations; what already applies today versus from 11 December 2027.

## Body sections

Scope determination; classification and route; architecture and data flows; risk assessment summary; SBOM and components; provision results (Annex I Part I, Part II, Article 13, Article 14, Annex II, Annex VII, Annex V); reporting readiness; adjudications; gaps; remediation plan; documentation drafts delivered; limitations and reliance; disclaimer.

## Appendices

Full requirement matrix; evidence index; scanner register; test-procedure traceability (legal text, procedure performed, evidence, limitations); adjudication register; ticket register; assessment manifest; standards status check record.

## Detailed gap structure

Gap ID and title; rating, status, confidence; provisions affected with plain-language source; legal exposure and date; affected components; finding; risk and implication; recommendation; implementation guidance; documentation change; retest criterion; evidence references; adjudication and ticket references.

## Mandatory deliverable formats

For `full`, `retest` and `evidence-pack`: `20-cra-readiness-report.md` (canonical), `.docx`, `.pdf`, and `21-report-rendering-manifest.json`. Same substantive content in all three; differences limited to presentation.

## Reliance wording

Conclusions are limited to the product version, scope, evidence and procedures as of the report date; may change with the product, its environment, integrations, the legal baseline or newly cited standards; do not guarantee the absence of vulnerabilities; do not bind any authority or notified body; and are not legal advice.
