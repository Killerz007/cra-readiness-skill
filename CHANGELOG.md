# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-06

- Initial public release.
- Pinned Official Journal texts from the EU Publications Office: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881 (text capture), with SHA-256 provenance and a monthly re-check workflow.
- Pinned Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 (27 July 2026) and the Commission FAQ as non-binding interpretive aids.
- Generated catalogue of 96 assessable provisions (Annex I Parts I and II, Articles 13 and 14, Annexes II, V and VII) and the Annex III and IV categories with technical descriptions.
- Interpretive assessment guide for every provision; methodology references for scope, roles, classification, conformity routes, standards status, risk assessment, SBOM, vulnerability handling, reporting, support period, substantial modification, technical documentation, open source and remote data processing.
- Scripts: official-text sync and parser, validator, assessment initialiser, scope and classification decisions, substantial-modification screening, SBOM checker, reporting-deadline calculator, support-period checker, regression comparator, ticket-queue exporter, evidence hasher.
- Templates: report, disclaimers, risk assessment, technical documentation, declaration drafts, user information, CVD policy, security.txt, advisory, reporting playbook and forms, support-period statement, registers, CI workflow.
- Schemas, fictional sample assessment, test suite and validation workflow.
