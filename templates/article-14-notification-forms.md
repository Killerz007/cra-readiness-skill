# Article 14 Notification Content Checklists

The legally required minimum content per stage (Article 14(2) and (4)). The ENISA single reporting platform defines the actual fields; keep these checklists aligned with the platform glossary.

## A. Actively exploited vulnerability

### A1. Early warning (within 24 hours of awareness)
- [ ] Manufacturer identity and contact; Assigned Representative submitting
- [ ] Product with digital elements affected (name, type, versions as known)
- [ ] Statement that an actively exploited vulnerability is being notified
- [ ] Member States on whose territory the product is known to have been made available (where applicable)
- [ ] Awareness timestamp and short basis
- [ ] Sensitivity indication; request to delay dissemination with cybersecurity-related grounds, if any

### A2. Vulnerability notification (within 72 hours of awareness; omit what was already provided)
- [ ] General information about the product concerned
- [ ] General nature of the exploit and of the vulnerability (CVE or EUVD identifier if available)
- [ ] Corrective or mitigating measures taken
- [ ] Corrective or mitigating measures that users can take
- [ ] How sensitive the manufacturer considers the notified information to be (where applicable)

### A3. Final report (no later than 14 days after a corrective or mitigating measure is available)
- [ ] Description of the vulnerability, including severity and impact
- [ ] Information on any malicious actor that has exploited or is exploiting it (where available)
- [ ] Details of the security update or other corrective measures made available

## B. Severe incident having an impact on the security of the product

### B1. Early warning (within 24 hours of awareness)
- [ ] Manufacturer identity and contact; Assigned Representative submitting
- [ ] Product affected
- [ ] Whether the incident is suspected of being caused by unlawful or malicious acts
- [ ] Member States on whose territory the product is known to have been made available (where applicable)
- [ ] Awareness timestamp and short basis
- [ ] Sensitivity indication; delay request if any

### B2. Incident notification (within 72 hours of awareness)
- [ ] General information about the nature of the incident
- [ ] Initial assessment of the incident
- [ ] Corrective or mitigating measures taken
- [ ] Corrective or mitigating measures that users can take
- [ ] Sensitivity indication (where applicable)

### B3. Final report (within one month after the incident notification)
- [ ] Detailed description of the incident, including severity and impact
- [ ] Type of threat or root cause likely to have triggered the incident
- [ ] Applied and ongoing mitigation measures

## C. User information (Article 14(8), both cases)
- [ ] Impacted users identified; decision whether all users are informed
- [ ] Content: the vulnerability or incident, risk mitigation and corrective measures users can deploy
- [ ] Format: structured, machine-readable where appropriate
- [ ] Timing and channel recorded
