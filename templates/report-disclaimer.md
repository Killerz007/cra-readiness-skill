# Approved report disclaimer language

## Full cover disclaimer

**Independent readiness assessment; limitation of reliance; no legal advice**

This report documents an independent readiness assessment of the product identified in this report against Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (the Cyber Resilience Act) as pinned at the legal baseline stated in this report. It is intended to help the manufacturer or other economic operator prepare for its own obligations under that Regulation.

This report is **not** a conformity assessment procedure under Annex VIII of the Regulation, not an EU-type examination or quality-system approval by a notified body, not a European cybersecurity certification, and not a determination by any market surveillance authority. It does not establish conformity with the Regulation, does not authorise the affixing of the CE marking, and does not constitute or replace the EU declaration of conformity, which the manufacturer alone draws up and signs under its sole responsibility.

This report is not legal advice. Determinations of scope, economic-operator role, product classification and applicable obligations are technical assessments of the facts provided against the text of the Regulation and the European Commission's non-binding guidance. Only the Court of Justice of the European Union can give an authoritative interpretation of Union law. Where this report recommends legal confirmation, the manufacturer should obtain it.

The preparer and any AI agent used to support this review are independent and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorised by, or acting on behalf of the European Commission, ENISA, any market surveillance authority, any notifying authority or notified body, or any standardisation organisation.

The procedures and conclusions in this report are limited to the product version, components, remote data processing solutions, environments, evidence, access and procedures identified as in scope and available during the assessment period, and to the legal baseline and the status of harmonised standards and secondary legislation recorded in this report. Changes to the product, its integrations, its deployment, the law, or cited harmonised standards after the report date may invalidate or materially affect the conclusions.

Security assessment is inherently limited. No review, automated scan, source-code review or test can guarantee that a product is free from vulnerabilities, weaknesses, misconfigurations or future compromise. Provisions recorded as Blocked, Not Assessed, out of scope or otherwise limited were not fully verified.

This report does not replace any notified-body report, certificate, contractual assurance, regulatory determination or independent professional opinion that may be required.
